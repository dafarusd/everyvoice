# Disclaimer — please read before relying on this

EveryVoice is free, and it is offered in good faith. It is also early, and
being honest about that is more useful to you than being encouraging.

## What this is not

**It is not a medical device.** It has not been reviewed, certified or
approved by any regulator, in any country. Nothing in it has been evaluated
by a clinical trial.

**It is not therapy, and it is not a speech-language pathologist.** AAC —
augmentative and alternative communication — works best when a qualified
professional helps choose the vocabulary, arranges it, and teaches the people
around the user how to model language on it. Decades of research say the
adults around a person matter more to whether AAC succeeds than which app
they use. This app removes a price barrier. It does not replace that person.

**It is not a substitute for professional assessment.** If you can get to a
speech-language pathologist, go. If you are waiting — and many people wait
months — this is meant to be something to use in the meantime, not instead.

## What has and has not been tested

As of version 0.1:

- The engine has 32 automated tests that pass.
- The app has been installed and driven on one phone, a Samsung Galaxy A15
  running Android 16. Speech, backup, restore, search, photo tiles and the
  missing-voice warning were each exercised there.
- **It has not been tested with a single AAC user.**
- **It has not been reviewed by a speech-language pathologist.**
- It has not been tested on a low-end or older device, which is the hardware
  it is aimed at.

No claim is made anywhere in this project that EveryVoice helps anyone
communicate better. That claim would need evidence this project does not yet
have.

## Do not rely on it in an emergency

Do not depend on this app — or any app — to call for help, report a medical
emergency, or communicate anything where failure would be dangerous. Phones
run out of battery, apps crash, and text-to-speech engines can be missing or
misconfigured. Keep a non-electronic way to communicate, and keep a way to
reach emergency services that does not depend on software.

## Your vocabulary is your responsibility to back up

A customised vocabulary can represent months of work. The app has a backup
feature and it is tested, but back up regularly and keep the file somewhere
that survives a lost or broken phone. If you uninstall the app, everything in
it is deleted.
